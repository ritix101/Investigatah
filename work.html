Standards of Evidence

Chain of custody

Every report published in this registry follows the same evidentiary standard. Findings are traceable, independently reproducible, and constructed exclusively from publicly observable data. This is what separates an investigation from an accusation.

STEP 01

Public Source Only

Evidence is drawn exclusively from public block explorers, on-chain transactions and the subject's own public statements. No private sources influence a published finding.

STEP 02

Independent Reproduction

Every wallet address, transaction signature and program interaction cited in a report can be independently reproduced by any reader using a standard block explorer.

STEP 03

Cross-Verification

On-chain findings are cross-checked against corporate filings, published terms of service and public statements. Contradictions are documented, not editorialised.

STEP 04

Publication & Archival

Reports are published to the public registry with a permanent case number and remain accessible in full. Amendments, if any, are recorded with clear dating.
