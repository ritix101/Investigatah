About the Unit

The ledger doesn't lie. Neither do we.

INVESTIGATAH is an independent blockchain forensics unit. We investigate crypto platforms, trace stolen funds, and publish what we find — with every claim anchored to public, verifiable on-chain evidence.

Why We Exist

Crypto crime counts on nobody looking

Rigged casinos hide behind "provably fair" badges. Fraud rings hide behind fresh wallets. Platforms enforce their own rules only when it protects the house. Meanwhile, victims are told there's nothing anyone can do.

There is. Every deposit, payout, and influencer payment is written permanently to a public ledger. Our job is to read it, connect it, and put it on record — so victims, exchanges, lawyers and law enforcement have something solid to act on.

Evidence over allegations

We don't publish theories. Every dossier is built on transaction IDs and wallet addresses anyone can independently verify on a block explorer.

Independence over access

We take no money from the platforms we investigate. No sponsorships, no affiliate codes, no "partnerships" — our only client is the truth of the chain.

Methodology

How an investigation runs

From first tip-off to published dossier — a disciplined, repeatable forensic process.

PHASE 01

Intake & Scoping

We collect transaction IDs, wallet addresses, screenshots and timelines from victims and whistleblowers, then define exactly what question the chain needs to answer.

PHASE 02

On-Chain Tracing

Funds are followed hop-by-hop across chains, bridges and mixers. Clustering and pattern analysis attribute wallets to entities — cold storage, hot wallets, payout routes.

PHASE 03

Cross-Verification

On-chain findings are checked against platform terms, corporate records, and public statements. Any claim that can't be independently verified gets cut.

PHASE 04

Dossier & Action

Findings are published as evidence-grade case files, and shared with exchanges and authorities where flagging, freezing or recovery is possible.

Operating Principles

What we hold ourselves to

Radical transparency

Our dossiers show the work — routes, addresses, amounts. Readers never have to take our word for it; they can replay the trace themselves.

Victim-first confidentiality

Public platforms get public scrutiny. Victims and sources get the opposite: strict confidentiality on every detail they share with us.

Proportional pressure

We escalate in order — documentation, notification, publication, referral. Platforms always get exactly the scrutiny their behavior earns.

Chain-agnostic coverage

Solana, Ethereum, Bitcoin, Tron and beyond. Fraud moves across chains; so do we.

Read the work, then judge us

Our published case files are the best introduction to how this unit operates. Every one is open, and every claim is checkable.
